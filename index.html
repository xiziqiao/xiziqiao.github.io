About / 02

A little more about me

I am a research scientist at Aether AI with an M.S. in Computer Science from UC San Diego. I enjoy problems that require both a clear research idea and the systems work to make that idea testable at scale.

My recent work studies how agents learn and act in complex environments. On the digital side, I build environments and tools for evaluating computer-use agents. On the embodied side, I study world models that can represent actions, geometry, and the consequences of change. Earlier work in ML systems and full-stack engineering still shapes how I approach reliability and performance.
